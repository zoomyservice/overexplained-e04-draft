Version 2, after your notes: smoother and slower arms, the whole body moving, the feet stepping and rolling, and a steady face. 16 s loop at 120 bpm, Workbench draft, no sound. Left = the hook angle down the aisle; right = closer.
  1. All the way through: knees bounce on every beat; his weight rocks from foot to foot every two beats, with the hips swinging and tilting over the standing leg and the chest rocking forward on the beat. The free foot rolls up onto its toes and takes a small side step, and the shoulders roll.
  2. 0–4 s, hands up: both hands in the air, swaying with his weight.
  3. 4–8 s, fist pumps: slow, half-time pumps on beats 1 and 3, the other arm grooving at his chest.
  4. 8–12 s, groove arms and the twist: loose fists at chest height swinging across his body; for these two bars both feet swivel on their toes with his hips.
  5. 12–16 s, one hand up: his right hand up, waving slowly side to side.
