E04 dance test (v4)

The new routine, built from your two reference clips. 8 s loop at 120 bpm, Workbench draft, no sound. Left = the hook angle down the aisle; right = closer.
  1. 0–2 s, arm flings: both arms thrown to one side on every beat (one up and out, one across the chest), the torso twisting and leaning into it, swinging through low between.
  2. 2–4 s, punches: fists punch up in turn twice a beat with a headbang, then a big V with the head thrown back.
  3. 4–6 s, low groove: steps out wide, sinks into a deep squat, hips swing side to side, raise-the-roof pushes.
  4. 6–8 s, sweep and spring: crouches and sweeps an arm down across to the other foot, then springs up into a punch, and again into a V.
The hook uses the first ~3.5 s (arm flings into punches) before he passes out. The feet stay planted, and the arms stay over the seated passengers' heads.
